Privacy Policy

TOC Factory on the web, and Factory Fun on iOS and Android

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What this covers

TOC Factory is a browser game that teaches the Theory of Constraints. Factory Fun is the same game in the iPhone, iPad, and Android apps. This policy covers all three. The game is made to run on your device and to keep what you do there.

What is kept on your device

Your nickname, the list of players on the device, levels finished and stars earned, your settings, course notes, free-play setups, and your exam answers, results, and certificate name are saved locally. In a browser this is the browser's local storage; in the apps it is the app's own local storage. On the web you can also type in your own factory's numbers in My factory; those stay on the device too.

There are no accounts. The game does not ask for your email address, a password, or your name. Its developer does not receive any of the information above.

Class mode (web only)

The web game lets you host or join a class. Class mode is not in the iOS or Android apps, and nothing is sent anywhere if you never host or join a class.

When you do use a class, the game connects to Firebase so everyone can see the class screen. Your device gets a random, anonymous ID from Firebase; it is not tied to your identity. While you are in a class, the game sends the nickname you typed, the level you are on, the levels you finished with their stars, and your answers, predictions, and run results. Only the host and the members of that class can see them, and a class deletes itself automatically after 12 hours.

Your course notes, your exam, and anything you type in My factory are never sent, even while you are in a class.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, tracking, or cookies.
  • No sale or sharing of your information for marketing.
  • No outside service other than Firebase for web class mode, described above. There are no other third-party connections.

Certificates

If you save or share a certificate, the file goes where you choose: a download from the browser, or your phone's own share sheet. The game does not see where you send it. The certificate number is worked out on your device.

Device backups

If you back up your device, the backup may include the game's saved data. That backup belongs to you and is under your control. The developer does not have a copy of it.

Deleting your data

  • On the web, clear the site data for TOC Factory in your browser's settings.
  • On iOS or Android, delete the app. Remove its backup copy in your backup settings as well if you use backups.
  • Class data disappears on its own after 12 hours.

Children

The game is meant for ages 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
